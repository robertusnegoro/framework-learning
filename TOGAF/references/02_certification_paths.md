# TOGAF Certification Paths

**Last Updated:** February 2025  
**Version:** TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition

---

## Overview

TOGAF certification is administered by **The Open Group** and delivered through **Pearson VUE** testing centers worldwide. Certifications are **vendor-neutral, globally recognized, and do not expire** — there are no renewal requirements.

There are two certification tracks: one based on TOGAF 9.2 and one based on the 10th Edition.

---

## TOGAF 9 Certification Track (Version 9.2)

### Level 1: TOGAF 9 Foundation

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Target Audience** | Newcomers to enterprise architecture |
| **Prerequisites** | None |
| **Exam** | Part 1 — 40 multiple-choice questions, 60 minutes |
| **Pass Mark** | 55% (22 out of 40) |
| **Format** | Closed book |
| **Topics** | TOGAF concepts, ADM overview, key terminology, Enterprise Continuum, architecture governance |
| **Cost** | ~$350 USD (exam only); ~$2,000–$3,500 with training |

### Level 2: TOGAF 9 Certified

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Target Audience** | Practitioners needing to apply TOGAF |
| **Prerequisites** | TOGAF 9 Foundation (Level 1) |
| **Exam** | Part 2 — 8 scenario-based questions, 90 minutes |
| **Pass Mark** | 60% |
| **Format** | Open book (TOGAF documentation provided) |
| **Topics** | Applying ADM, architecture governance, content framework, real-world scenarios |
| **Cost** | ~$350 USD (exam only) |

> [!TIP]
> A **combined exam** (Part 1 + Part 2) is available for candidates who want to achieve Level 2 in a single sitting. Cost is ~$550 USD.

## TOGAF 10th Edition Certification Track

### Level 1: TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Foundation

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Target Audience** | Newcomers and practitioners transitioning to the 10th Edition |
| **Prerequisites** | None |
| **Exam** | TOGAF EA Part 1 |
| **Topics** | Core concepts, common language, framework structure, ADM fundamentals |
| **Format** | Multiple-choice, closed book |

### Level 2: TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Practitioner

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Target Audience** | Practitioners who apply TOGAF in EA use cases |
| **Prerequisites** | TOGAF EA Foundation (Level 1) |
| **Exam** | TOGAF EA Part 2 |
| **Topics** | Applying TOGAF for various EA scenarios, configuring the framework |
| **Format** | Scenario-based, open book |

### Bridge Exam: TOGAF 9 → TOGAF 10

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Target Audience** | Existing TOGAF 9 Certified holders |
| **Exam** | OGEA-10B |
| **Outcome** | TOGAF EA Practitioner (10th Edition) |
| **Topics** | Changes in the 10th Edition, modular structure, updated ADM |

## Study Strategy

### For Foundation Level

1. **Read the TOGAF Fundamental Content** — available free at [pubs.opengroup.org](https://pubs.opengroup.org/togaf-standard/)
2. **Study the materials in this repo** — `01_foundation/` covers all Foundation topics
3. **Focus on:** ADM phases (names, sequence, purpose), key terminology, Enterprise Continuum, four domains
4. **Practice exams** — available from official training providers and platforms like Udemy and WhizLabs
5. **Time management** — 60 minutes for 40 questions = 1.5 minutes per question

### For Certified / Practitioner Level

1. **Deep dive into ADM** — understand inputs, outputs, and key activities for each phase
2. **Study the materials in this repo** — `02_intermediate/` covers detailed ADM and domains
3. **Practice scenario-based questions** — focus on *applying* concepts, not just memorizing
4. **Review the TOGAF content framework** — understand deliverables, artifacts, and building blocks
5. **Use the open-book advantage wisely** — know where to find information quickly

### Recommended Study Timeline

| Certification | Self-Study | With Training Course |
|--------------|-----------|---------------------|
| Foundation | 3-4 weeks | 2-3 days classroom + 1 week review |
| Certified / Practitioner | 4-6 weeks | 2-3 days classroom + 2 weeks review |
| Bridge (9 → 10) | 1-2 weeks | 1 day classroom + 1 week review |

---

## Key Takeaways

- Two tracks exist: **TOGAF 9.2** and **TOGAF 10th Edition** — both are currently valid
- Certifications **do not expire** — no renewal required
- Foundation is closed-book MCQ; Certified/Practitioner is open-book scenario-based
- Existing TOGAF 9 holders can **bridge to 10th Edition** via OGEA-10B exam
- **TOGAF Business Architecture** is a specialist certification for business-focused architects
